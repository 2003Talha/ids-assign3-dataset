Chinese Corn Soup with Chicken

Cuisine: Chinese

Prep Time: 10 minutes

Cook Time: 20 minutes

Total Time: 30 minutes

Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 2 cups creamed corn (canned)
  • 200g boneless chicken breast, finely shredded
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 2 eggs, lightly beaten
  • 2 tablespoons cornstarch mixed with 3 tablespoons water
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil
  • Salt and white pepper to taste
  • 2 spring onions, sliced

Steps

  1. Bring chicken broth to a boil in a large pot.
  2. Add creamed corn and stir well. Simmer for 5 minutes.
  3. Add shredded chicken and cook for another 5 minutes.
  4. Stir in the cornstarch mixture gradually to thicken the soup.
  5. Slowly pour beaten eggs into the soup in a thin stream while stirring.
  6. Season with salt, white pepper, and sesame oil.
  7. Garnish with spring onions and serve hot.

Tips

For a vegetarian version, omit chicken and use vegetable broth. Add tofu for protein. This soup is gluten-free if you use tamari instead of soy sauce.
