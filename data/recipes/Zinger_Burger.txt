Zinger Burger Recipe

Cuisine: American / Fast Food
Prep Time: 20 minutes
Cook Time: 15 minutes
Total Time: 35 minutes
Servings: 4

Ingredients:
1. 4 boneless chicken breasts
2. 1 cup buttermilk
3. 1 cup all-purpose flour
4. 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper
5. 1 teaspoon garlic powder
6. 1 teaspoon paprika
7. Salt and pepper to taste
8. Oil for deep frying
9. 4 burger buns
10. Lettuce, tomato, mayonnaise for serving

Steps:
1. Marinate chicken in buttermilk with salt and pepper for at least 1 hour.
2. Mix flour, cayenne pepper, garlic powder, and paprika in a bowl.
3. Coat each chicken piece in the seasoned flour mixture.
4. Heat oil in a deep pan to 180 C (350 F).
5. Deep fry chicken for 12 to 15 minutes until golden and cooked through.
6. Drain on paper towels.
7. Toast burger buns lightly.
8. Assemble with lettuce, tomato, fried chicken, and mayonnaise.
9. Serve immediately and enjoy
