RECIPE 1: Classic Margherita Pizza
-----------------------------------
Cuisine: Italian
Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 12 minutes
Total Time: 27 minutes
Servings: 2

Ingredients:
- 1 pizza dough ball (store-bought or homemade)
- 1/2 cup tomato sauce
- 150g fresh mozzarella
- Fresh basil leaves
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 250°C (480°F).
2. Roll out pizza dough on a floured surface.
3. Spread tomato sauce over the base.
4. Tear mozzarella and distribute evenly.
5. Bake for 10–12 minutes until crust is golden.
6. Top with fresh basil, drizzle olive oil, and serve.

RECIPE 2: Gluten-Free Banana Bread
------------------------------------
Cuisine: American
Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 55 minutes
Total Time: 65 minutes
Servings: 8 slices
Dietary: Gluten-Free

Ingredients:
- 3 ripe bananas, mashed
- 2 eggs
- 1/3 cup melted coconut oil
- 1/4 cup honey or maple syrup
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1.5 cups almond flour
- 1/2 cup gluten-free oats

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 175°C (350°F). Grease a loaf pan.
2. Mix mashed bananas, eggs, coconut oil, honey, and vanilla in a bowl.
3. Add baking soda and salt. Mix well.
4. Fold in almond flour and oats until combined.
5. Pour into prepared loaf pan.
6. Bake for 55–60 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean.
7. Cool for 10 minutes before slicing.

RECIPE 3: Roasted Vegetables
------------------------------
Cuisine: Mediterranean
Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 25 minutes
Total Time: 35 minutes
Servings: 4
Dietary: Vegan, Gluten-Free

Ingredients:
- 2 bell peppers, diced
- 2 zucchinis, sliced
- 1 red onion, quartered
- 200g cherry tomatoes
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh parsley for garnish

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 220°C (425°F).
2. Chop all vegetables into bite-sized pieces.
3. Toss with olive oil, oregano, salt, and pepper.
4. Spread in a single layer on a baking sheet.
5. Roast for 20–25 minutes, turning halfway through.
6. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve.

RECIPE 4: Chocolate Lava Cake
-------------------------------
Cuisine: French
Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 12 minutes
Total Time: 27 minutes
Servings: 4
Dietary: Vegetarian
Tags: dessert, chocolate, no eggs alternative below

Ingredients:
- 200g dark chocolate
- 100g butter
- 3 eggs + 3 egg yolks
- 80g sugar
- 60g all-purpose flour
- Pinch of salt
- Cocoa powder for dusting
- Vanilla ice cream for serving

Steps:
1. Preheat oven to 200°C (390°F). Butter and dust 4 ramekins with cocoa.
2. Melt chocolate and butter together over a double boiler.
3. Whisk eggs, yolks, and sugar until pale.
4. Fold chocolate mixture into eggs.
5. Sift in flour and salt. Fold gently.
6. Pour into ramekins and bake 10–12 minutes.
7. Edges should be set but center still soft.
8. Invert onto plates, dust with cocoa, serve with ice cream.

Egg-Free Variant: Replace eggs with flax eggs (1 tbsp flaxseed + 3 tbsp water each).
